About Zafira / Mengenali Zafira

Zafira Amelia has Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). She has her own strengths, communication style, sensory preferences, and support needs.

Zafira ialah individu yang unik. Berikan ruang untuk dia berkomunikasi, memproses maklumat, dan bertindak dengan caranya sendiri.

Communication / Komunikasi

  • Short phrases and unique physical gestures may be part of how she communicates.
  • She can process spoken words but may need 5–10 seconds before responding.
  • Clear, concrete and literal language works best.
  • Pause and wait patiently instead of repeating questions too quickly.

Cara berkomunikasi

  • Gunakan arahan yang jelas, mudah, khusus dan terus kepada maksud.
  • Berikan masa tambahan untuk dia memproses dan memberi respons.

Sensory Needs / Keperluan Sensori

  • She may be sensitive to sudden loud noises.
  • When overwhelmed, she may hand-flap, sing, or pace as ways of coping or self-regulating.
  • Lower surrounding noise and allow physical space.

Keperluan deria

  • Zafira mungkin sensitif terhadap bunyi kuat yang datang secara tiba-tiba.
  • Apabila terbeban, dia mungkin mengepakkan tangan, menyanyi atau berjalan berulang-alik.

Routines & Changes / Rutin & Perubahan

  • Predictable routines, clear structure, and visual schedules can help her feel secure.
  • Unexpected schedule changes may cause distress.
  • Explain changes calmly and as early as possible.

Rutin dan perubahan

  • Rutin yang boleh dijangka dan jadual bergambar boleh membantu.
  • Terangkan perubahan dengan tenang dan seawal mungkin.

My Strengths / Kekuatan Zafira

🎵 Singing / Menyanyi

Singing is one of Zafira’s strengths and can be a positive way for her to express herself.

Menyanyi merupakan salah satu kekuatan Zafira dan boleh menjadi cara yang positif untuk dia mengekspresikan dirinya.

How You Can Help / Cara Anda Boleh Membantu

  • Positive reinforcement: Give immediate, specific praise.
  • Calm voice: Use a quiet, steady and neutral tone.
  • Physical space: Do not crowd, grab, or force contact.
  • Comfort items: A mobile phone or tablet may help her regulate when used appropriately.
  • Processing time: Pause and wait before expecting an answer.

Petua ringkas

  • Gunakan pengukuhan positif dan pujian yang segera.
  • Kurangkan bunyi persekitaran, beri ruang dan bercakap dengan tenang.

Kindness, patience and understanding matter.

Kesabaran, kebaikan dan kefahaman boleh membuat perubahan yang besar.
